# Rational AG — two-minute pitch (about 300 words)

**Sell Rational, 12-month target €414, 27% below today's €566.50.**

Rational is one of the best businesses in European capital goods. It has about half of the world market for combi-steamers. It earned a 26% EBIT margin last year, has a 65% return on invested capital and holds €425 million of net cash. I'm not arguing the business is weak. I'm arguing the share price already pays for all of it, and then some.

Three points.

First, growth has normalised. Sales grew 6% in 2024, 5.5% in 2025 and 5.8% in the first half of this year. China fell 29% last year, Asia is still shrinking, and US tariffs are costing about a point of gross margin. My base case is about 6% a year to 2030, a little below consensus.

Second, the discount rate has changed. From 2016 to 2021 the ten-year Bund yielded around zero; today it yields 3.55%. Over the last five years Rational's shares moved 1.7 times as much as the STOXX Europe 600. With a Blume-adjusted beta and Damodaran's 4.2% risk premium, the cost of equity is 9.8%. At today's price the market is discounting at 7.2%, as if this were a low-risk stock.

Third, the valuation doesn't need heroic assumptions to fail. At 9.8%, the price needs either 15% sales growth every year to 2030 or a 38% EBIT margin, against a ten-year high of 27%. My bull case, 28% margins and nearly 7% growth for a decade, is worth €485, still below the share price. 24 of the 25 cells in my sensitivity table say Sell.

The risk to the call is that investors keep treating Rational as defensive and rates fall. The catalysts are the nine-month figures on 5 November, the capital markets day on 19 November and 2027 guidance in March.

Great company, wrong price: Sell, target €414.
